# OpenClaw Legal Templates for Vaultr

## Connection to MiroFish
Legal templates are referenced in the Execution Vault page at `src/app/(dashboard)/vault/page.tsx`.

## Templates Available
1. **Investment Agreement** - Standard deal structuring template
2. **Terms of Service** - Platform usage terms
3. **Privacy Policy** - POPIA-compliant privacy policy for South African users
4. **NDA Template** - Non-disclosure for deal marketplace discussions

## Usage
Templates are displayed in the Vault page under "Legal Templates" section.
Users can download and customize for their deals.
