# Vaultr Privacy Policy

**Last Updated**: [DATE]

## 1. INTRODUCTION
Vaultr (Pty) Ltd ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

## 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### 2.1 Account Information
- Full name, email address
- Country of residence
- Investment experience level
- Risk tolerance preferences

### 2.2 Financial Information
- Portfolio holdings (user-entered)
- Trading history (simulated and tracked)
- Subscription and payment data
- Capital range preferences

### 2.3 Usage Data
- AI query history
- Feature usage patterns
- Device and browser information
- IP address

## 3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
- Provide and personalize the service
- AI memory system for better recommendations
- Subscription management and billing
- Security monitoring and fraud prevention
- Service improvement and analytics

## 4. DATA STORAGE & SECURITY
- Data stored on Supabase (PostgreSQL) with Row Level Security
- Encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3)
- Google Cloud backup (GStack) for document vault
- Regular security audits and penetration testing

## 5. DATA SHARING
We do NOT sell your personal data. We share data only with:
- Payment processors (Stripe, Stitch) for billing
- AI providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Perplexity) for analysis (anonymized queries)
- Law enforcement when legally required

## 6. YOUR RIGHTS (POPIA)
- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
- **Correction**: Update inaccurate information
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your data
- **Objection**: Object to processing of your data
- **Portability**: Export your data in standard format

## 7. COOKIES
We use essential cookies for authentication and preferences. No third-party tracking cookies.

## 8. DATA RETENTION
- Active accounts: data retained while account exists
- Deleted accounts: data purged within 30 days
- Legal obligations: financial records retained 5 years per SARS requirements

## 9. INFORMATION OFFICER
For POPIA requests: privacy@vaultr.co.za

## 10. CHANGES
We may update this policy. Users notified via email for material changes.
